## FIX 1 — CAMERA BUTTON DOCKED INTO THE NAV BAR

**Current problem:** the camera button sits below the bar, partly outside it.

**Required result**
- The camera button is a child of the same container as the bar (or is positioned relative to the bar's measured top edge), so its position depends on the bar, not on a fixed offset.
- It is centered horizontally (its center x equals the screen width divided by two).
- It is 72dp in diameter, with its top rising about 22dp above the bar's top edge, and a 5dp ring cut-out in the screen background color.
- The bar stays a rounded floating container, inset 16dp from each side, 76dp tall, above the Android gesture area.
- Measure with `onLayout` in development. If the button's center y is more than 2dp away from the bar's top edge plus 0, log a `__DEV__` warning naming the component and the measured values, and fix the cause.

## FIX 2 — "SCAN" LABEL VISIBLE

- The "Scan" label must use the accent text token (`accentText`), not white.
- Place it directly under the camera icon, inside the bar, so it is above the bottom edge of the bar and fully visible.
- Check contrast against the bar's `surface` color in both themes. If it fails, use the text token instead.

## FIX 3 — REMOVE THE STRAY GEAR

- The gray circular gear floating over the document card is a leftover. Find it and delete it, not just hide it.
- Search the project for any settings button that uses absolute positioning, a floating-action style, or a second gear icon outside the Home header (search for `settings` in `components/` and in `app/(tabs)/index.tsx`).
- Keep exactly one settings entry point: the gear in the Home header, aligned with the title, which opens Settings.

## FIX 4 — REDUCE THE TOP SPACE

**Current problem:** the title sits about 60dp below the top of the screen, which looks empty.

**Required result**
- The header's top padding is the device's top safe-area inset plus **12dp**. Nothing else adds space above the title.
- The title and the storage line keep their current size and spacing.
- The toggle and everything below it move up with the header. Keep the gap between the header and the toggle at 16dp.
- Check small phones (360dp wide) and tall phones (411dp wide, 800dp+ tall).

## FIX 5 — AD SLOT DIRECTLY ABOVE THE NAV BAR

**Current problem:** the banner sits in the middle of the screen, far above the bar.

**Required result**
- The ad slot's bottom edge is **12dp above the top of the nav bar's container** (not the top of the camera button, which can rise above the bar). Compute this from the bar's measured height and the safe-area bottom inset, not from a fixed number.
- The slot's height is reserved so the list doesn't jump when the ad loads.
- The slot must not overlap the camera button, the document list, or the folder grid.
- The banner stays on the Home tab only, as before.
