## FIX 1 — BOTTOM BAR (fuller look, bigger camera)

**Current problem:** the bar is a thin strip with three small items and a lot of empty white space, so it looks unfinished.

**Required design: a raised, rounded, floating bar**
- The bar is a rounded container (full-radius, about 28dp corner radius) inset **16dp** from the left and right screen edges, with its bottom sitting above the Android gesture area (use the safe-area bottom inset plus 8dp).
- Height: **76dp**. Background: the `surface` token (white in light theme, `surface` in dark). A 1px `border` token outline and a soft shadow, so it reads as a separate floating layer above the content.
- Three items inside the bar: **Home** (left), **Tools** (right), and the **camera** in the center. Home and Tools are evenly spaced on either side of the center.
- Each tab item: 24dp icon with a 12sp label underneath. The active tab uses the accent color on both icon and label, with a soft accent-tinted pill behind the icon.

**Bigger camera button**
- Diameter **72dp** (up from 60dp), filled with the accent color `#2563EB`, white camera icon at 30dp.
- It sits in the exact horizontal center of the bar and rises about **22dp above** the top edge of the bar.
- Give it a **ring cut-out**: a 5dp ring in the screen background color around the button, so it looks docked into the bar rather than floating on top of it.
- Add a small **"Scan"** label (12sp, semibold, accent color) directly under the camera icon **inside the button area**, or just below the button in the bar, whichever fits without clipping. This fills the empty space and tells the user what the button does.
- Press feedback: scale to 96% over 100ms, and back.
- Keep the bar hidden on the camera, editor, share, and viewer screens, as it is now.

**Why this layout:** a floating, rounded bar with a larger docked center button looks finished and balanced, and the "Scan" label gives the empty middle of the bar a clear purpose without adding a fourth tab.

**Layout rules**
- Measure the camera button's center with `onLayout` and confirm it equals the bar's width divided by two. Keep the existing `__DEV__` check that logs a mismatch.
- The banner's reserved slot sits above the bar. Update its position math so the banner never overlaps the raised camera button (leave at least 12dp between the banner and the top of the camera button).
- The content above the bar has enough bottom padding that the last list item is never hidden behind the bar.
