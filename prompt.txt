## FIX 1 — NAV BAR AND CAMERA BUTTON POSITION

**Current problem (from the owner's screenshot):** the camera button sits at the far left edge, partly cut off by the screen edge, instead of centered in the bar. It overlaps the bar's top edge in the wrong place. The Home, Tools, and Settings tabs are not balanced around it.

**Required result:**
- **Layout decision (recommended, Option B):** the bar has **two tabs on each side of a centered camera button**: **Home** on the left, **Tools** on the right. The camera sits exactly in the horizontal center of the bar. Settings leaves the bar and returns to the **gear icon in the header**, which the owner already has. This is the only layout that stays symmetric with the camera in the center.
  - Build the bar as three columns with equal widths: the left column holds Home, the center column holds the camera (centered in that column, so its center x equals the screen width divided by two), and the right column holds Tools.
  - Keep the Settings screen and its route. The gear opens the same Settings screen.
  - If the owner chooses Option A instead (keep three tabs and place the camera at the right end of the bar), follow that instead and report it. Do not mix the two options.
- The camera button is a round accent button (`#2563EB`, white camera icon, about 60dp diameter). It rises about 16 to 20dp above the top edge of the bar, with a soft shadow. It is fully visible and never clipped by the screen edge.
- **Measure, don't guess.** Position the camera button with `left: 50%` and `marginLeft: -buttonWidth / 2`, or with a flex row whose center item is the button, and confirm with `onLayout` that its center x equals the screen width divided by two. Test on a 360dp-wide layout and a 411dp-wide layout.
- The bar's bottom padding uses the safe-area bottom inset, so nothing sits under the Android gesture area.
- The bar keeps its fixed height. Content above it is never pushed by the banner or the camera button.
- Keep the existing hide rules: the bar is hidden on the camera, editor, share, and viewer screens.
- Keep the current active-tab accent color and icons. Only the spacing and alignment change.

**Acceptance check:** on the Home screen the camera button is centered horizontally with equal space on both sides, fully visible, and the banner above the bar doesn't cover it.

## FIX 2 — MAKE THE BUTTONS LOOK DESIGNED

**Current problem:** the All files and Folders toggle, the sort button, the folder tiles, and the bottom bar look plain and generic. The owner wants them to look finished, while keeping the same layout and behavior.

Use the design skills listed in AGENTS.md (react-native-design for styling, emil-design-eng for motion). Apply these specific rules:

**Segmented toggle (All files / Folders)**
- One pill-shaped container with a subtle surface background (`surface` token) and a 1px border (`border` token). Padding 4dp inside.
- The selected segment is a filled pill in the accent color with white text, with a short slide or fade animation (120 to 180ms) when switching.
- The unselected segment shows muted text and a small icon (a grid icon for All files, a folder icon for Folders). The same icon set is used everywhere.
- Equal widths, full content width, 44dp minimum height.

**Sort button**
- A small pill with a sort icon and the current option label. Use the same pill style as the toggle, with a lighter background when idle.
- Opens the existing sort menu, unchanged.

**Primary buttons (for example Add scans, Create folder)**
- Filled accent (`#2563EB`), white text, 14dp corner radius, 52dp height, with a pressed state that darkens slightly and scales to 98% over 100ms.
- Disabled state uses the muted surface color, with no shadow.

**Secondary buttons (for example Skip for now, Cancel)**
- Outlined with the `border` token, accent text, same height and corner radius as primary.

**Folder tiles**
- Keep the 2-column grid and the dashed "New folder" tile.
- Each tile: 16dp corner radius, a soft icon badge at the top-left (accent tint background, accent folder icon), the folder name in semibold, and the scan count in muted text. A subtle border and no heavy shadow.
- Pressed state: slight scale to 98%.

**Bottom bar tabs**
- Active tab: accent icon and label, with a soft accent-tinted pill behind the icon.
- Inactive tabs: muted icon and label.
- Labels are 12sp, icons 24dp. Keep the spacing even.

**Across all of these**
- Use only theme tokens from `constants/theme.ts`. No hard-coded hex values in components.
- Keep corner radii consistent: 14dp for buttons, 16dp for cards and tiles, full round for pills and the camera button.
- Make sure text is still readable in both light and dark themes, and check the accent color on text against both backgrounds.
- Don't add new fonts or icon libraries. Use the icon set already in the project.
